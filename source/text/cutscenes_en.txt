THE KING OF FIGHTERS XI INTERNATIONAL -- CUTSCENE SUBTITLES, ENGLISH: how the script was brought over

The English subtitles are the script of the North American release, which is SNK's and is not copied here.
It was matched to the Japanese disc's 842 subtitle records, 841 of which take a line of it.
Nothing was reworded. What was done to fit the Japanese disc's subtitle records:

  - 65 lines of three rows are broken into two rows (the disc never shows three);
  - 1 line that covers two records on the disc is split at its own break;
  - 3 records hold two speakers, one a row: each takes two lines of the script;
  - 5 thoughts keep the disc's ( ) around the script's words, where the script uses quotes;
  - one misplaced space in the script is set right;
  - 1 record has no line in the script and keeps the disc's.

The records this touched, by file and record number:

  one US line over two records: MIDDLE#3, MIDDLE#4
  two speakers in one record: two US lines: END03#4, END05#9, END06#27
  a thought: the US words in the disc's ( ): END01#28, END02#5, END09#16, END10#9, END10#10
  no US line: SNK's stays: END03#36
